3D Story Studio

3D Story Studio is a small publishing tool run by an independent creator. It takes short 3D story videos that we make ourselves and publishes them to our own social accounts on a set schedule.

Our channels

  • Psych Lab 3D: famous psychology experiments, recreated in 3D. Instagram · YouTube · TikTok
  • Wise Crowd, Mad Crowd: true stories of crowds at their best and worst, rebuilt in 3D. Instagram · YouTube

How it works with TikTok

  1. The account owner signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and approves the requested permissions.
  2. When a new video is finished, the tool shows the title, caption and privacy setting, then uploads the video file to the owner's TikTok account through the Content Posting API.
  3. The tool checks the publishing status and records the result. It never posts to anyone else's account.
