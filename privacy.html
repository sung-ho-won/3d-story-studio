Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy explains how 3D Story Studio ("the tool") handles information.

1. Information we use

  • Access tokens issued by TikTok and other platforms after the account owner authorizes the tool.
  • Basic account information (such as the account's display name and open ID) to confirm which of our accounts is connected.
  • Publishing results (such as a post ID, status and time) to keep our own posting log.

We do not collect information about viewers, followers or any other TikTok users.

2. How we use it

Only to upload our own videos to our own accounts and to check that each upload succeeded.

3. Storage and sharing

Tokens and logs are stored only on the operator's own computer. We do not sell, rent or share this information with anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the account stays connected. Revoking access in TikTok's settings ends the tool's access, and we then delete the stored token.

5. Children

The tool is not directed to children and is used only by its adult operator.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
